About Fairfax Food Grades

Important information about this community resource.

Not Affiliated with Government

This website is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, funded by, or endorsed by the Fairfax County Government, the Fairfax County Health Department, or any of its agencies. Grades shown here are not official county ratings, and the county does not issue letter grades.

Built from Public Records

Grades are calculated by this site from public records: the inspection reports the Fairfax County Health Department publishes for permitted food establishments, and health complaint records from the county's PLUS portal. Reports are read automatically, and automated reading can make mistakes. Records may also be incomplete or out of date, since the county updates its data on its own schedule and removes reports after about two years. We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any information provided.

Every inspection listed links to the county's original report. When this site and the county's report differ, the county's report is the authoritative record.

Use at Your Own Risk

A grade summarizes how a place has done on routine inspections. It is not a guarantee that food is safe, and not a prediction that it is unsafe. A single inspection is a snapshot of one visit, and conditions can change at any time. Complaints listed on this site are allegations: the county does not publish what they were about or whether they were confirmed. Any decision you make using this website is entirely at your own risk. Please read the underlying reports, and use your own judgment.

Your Privacy

This site has no accounts and sets no tracking cookies. If you use Near me, your browser shares your location with this page only to sort results by distance; it stays on your device and is never sent to us or stored.

Our Mission

This product is intended to help anyone in the County make more informed choices about where they eat, by making public inspection records easier to find and understand. The county's own viewer shows only whether an inspection passed; the reports themselves say much more. Our goal is to make that information accessible, and to explain openly how every grade is calculated.
